Just Tap

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

1. Information We Process

Just Tap may process information required to provide gameplay, rankings, social features, clans, progression, cosmetics, anti-cheat protection, and advertising.

  • A randomly generated player identifier
  • A player authentication token used to identify your Just Tap profile
  • Your chosen username
  • Tap counts and gameplay statistics
  • Rank and progression information
  • Achievement and cosmetic data
  • Friends and social connections
  • Clan membership and clan activity
  • Profile picture data, if you choose to upload a profile picture
  • Gameplay timing data used for anti-cheat detection
  • Suspicious activity and anti-cheat signals
  • A one-way hashed device identifier used for fraud and abuse prevention

Just Tap does not require you to create an account using your real name, email address, or password.

2. Why We Process This Information

This information is used to:

  • Save and synchronize gameplay progress
  • Display rankings and leaderboards
  • Provide friends and clan features
  • Unlock and equip cosmetics
  • Maintain player progression and achievements
  • Detect cheating, automation, abuse, and manipulation of competitive features
  • Protect other players and the integrity of rankings
  • Restore and maintain your Just Tap player profile
  • Display advertising where applicable

3. Profile Pictures and Photo Access

Just Tap only requests photo library access when you choose to select a profile picture. Photo access is not required for normal gameplay.

If you choose a profile picture, the selected image may be stored with your Just Tap player profile so it can be displayed in the app.

4. Anti-Cheat and Device Information

Just Tap uses anti-cheat systems to protect competitive gameplay.

These systems may process:

  • Tap speed
  • Tap timing patterns
  • Repetitive or machine-like input patterns
  • Suspicious gameplay activity
  • A hashed device identifier

The device identifier is processed as a one-way hash and is used to help detect repeated abuse, cheating, and attempts to bypass enforcement. Anti-cheat information may be used to restrict or block access to competitive features when abuse is detected.

5. Advertising

Just Tap uses Google Mobile Ads / Google AdMob to display advertising.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as:

  • Device information
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Approximate location information
  • Ad interaction data
  • Consent and privacy choices

Advertising behavior may depend on your region, device settings, and the choices presented in Google's consent flow.

6. Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required, Just Tap may display a consent interface before personalized advertising or related data processing takes place. Your advertising choices may also be affected by your iOS privacy settings and other device-level controls.

7. Data Storage

Online player and gameplay information is stored using backend infrastructure provided by Supabase. Local gameplay and settings data may also be stored directly on your device using local application storage.

Data may be processed on servers located outside your country depending on the infrastructure used by service providers.

8. Data Retention

Just Tap keeps information for as long as necessary to provide the app's functionality, maintain rankings, prevent abuse, and operate your player profile.

Anti-cheat and fraud-prevention information may be retained where necessary to prevent repeated abuse or attempts to evade restrictions.

9. Deleting Your Data

Just Tap includes a Delete My Data option in the Privacy & Data section of the app.

Using this option may permanently delete applicable:

  • Player profile information
  • Tap and gameplay statistics
  • Rank and progression data
  • Friends
  • Clan-related player data
  • Cosmetics
  • Profile image data
  • Anti-cheat account data associated with the player

The app also resets applicable locally stored gameplay and profile data. Deletion cannot be undone. After deletion, a new Just Tap player profile may be created.

10. Children

Just Tap is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children beyond the data necessary to operate the application. Users should comply with the minimum age requirements applicable in their country and with the requirements of the relevant app store.

11. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect player information and reduce unauthorized access, manipulation, and abuse. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

12. Third-Party Services

Just Tap relies on third-party services that may independently process information according to their own privacy policies.

  • Google AdMob
  • Supabase
  • Apple App Store and related Apple services

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Just Tap changes its features, data practices, service providers, or legal requirements. The latest version will be made available on this page.
